# The Miracle on Ice Game and Context

Created: 2026-02-26 20:10:03
Tags: sports, history, Cold War

---

The 'Miracle on Ice' refers to the medal-round game on February 22, 1980, where the U.S. defeated the four-time defending gold medalist Soviet Union team.
Historical Context:
- Cold War tensions (Soviet invasion of Afghanistan).
- The Soviet team was essentially professional and had dominated international hockey for decades.
- The U.S. had lost 10-3 to the Soviets in an exhibition game just days before the Olympics.
The Game:
- The U.S. trailed 3-2 entering the third period.
- Mark Johnson tied it; Mike Eruzione scored the go-ahead goal with 10 minutes left.
- Al Michaels' famous call: 'Do you believe in miracles? YES!'
