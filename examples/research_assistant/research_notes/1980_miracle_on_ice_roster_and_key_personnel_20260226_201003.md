# 1980 Miracle on Ice Roster and Key Personnel

Created: 2026-02-26 20:10:03
Tags: sports, history, hockey, 1980 Olympics

---

The 1980 U.S. Olympic Men's Ice Hockey team, coached by Herb Brooks, consisted entirely of amateur and collegiate players. 
Key Roster Members:
- Mike Eruzione (Captain, scored the winning goal against USSR)
- Jim Craig (Goaltender, made 36 saves against USSR)
- Mark Johnson (Leading scorer)
- Ken Morrow (Defenseman, later won 4 Stanley Cups with NY Islanders)
- Mark Pavelich (Key playmaker)

The team's average age was 21, making them the youngest team in U.S. Olympic history and the tournament.
